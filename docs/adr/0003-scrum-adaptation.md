# ADR-0003: Scrum adapted for a solo developer

- **Status:** Accepted (supervisor role pending confirmation)
- **Date:** 2026-11-11

## Context

The capstone is developed by one person and follows Scrum as a process. Full Scrum roles and
ceremonies do not fit a team of one.

## Decision

- The student is the Developer (and Scrum Master).
- The supervisor acts as Product Owner; this is pending the supervisor's confirmation.
- One sprint equals one phase of `CAPSTONE_PROCEDURES.md`; sprint length is to be confirmed.
- Ceremonies are scaled down to a written note per sprint in `docs/sprints/` (Goal, Planned, Done,
  Review, Retrospective) instead of live meetings.
- The product backlog is the task list of each phase; the project board is kept by the human.

## Consequences

- Scrum artifacts exist for the supervisor and the report with little overhead.
- Review quality depends on the supervisor's availability.
